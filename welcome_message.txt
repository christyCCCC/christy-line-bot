歡迎進入 Christy P 的藝術世界。

《萬物可愛論》凝視那些人們習慣隱藏的部分——慾望、嫉妒、孤獨、恐懼與愛。

在女孩、黑貓、怪物與皇冠構成的世界裡，情緒不再被簡單劃分為善或惡，而是以另一種樣貌，重新被看見。

對 Christy P 而言，「可愛」並非裝飾，也不代表純真或無害。它是一種觀看的方法，讓我們越過第一印象，凝視人性之中的柔軟、矛盾，以及那些難以被命名的部分。

「我沒有把世界變可愛，
只是看見了萬物可愛的那一面。」

— Christy P

Welcome to the world of Christy P.

EVERYTHING IS CUTE turns toward the parts of ourselves we tend to hide—desire, envy, loneliness, fear, and love.

Within a universe inhabited by girls, black cats, monsters, and crowns, these emotions are no longer reduced to good or evil. Instead, they are allowed to be seen anew.

For Christy P, cuteness is neither ornament nor a sign of innocence or harmlessness. It is a way of seeing—one that moves beyond first impressions to reveal the tenderness, contradictions, and unnamed complexities of human nature.

“I don’t make the world cute.
I simply see the cute side of it.”

— Christy P
